Design a clean minimalist editorial personal blog inspired by modern
independent writers and literary magazines.
 
Use a warm off-white background, dark charcoal typography, generous whitespace,
a narrow reading column, elegant serif headings, clean sans-serif UI text,
minimal borders, no gradients, no heavy shadows, and no excessive card layouts.
 
The design should feel thoughtful, intellectual, calm, premium, and content-first.
Create a simple top navigation, vertical article archive, readable long-form
article pages, category archives, search, newsletter section, and a restrained footer.
